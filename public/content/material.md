Books, notes, and reviews that complement the paper bibliography.

## Books

**[Hypothesis Testing with E-values](https://web.stanford.edu/~aramdas/ebook-final.pdf)** — Aaditya Ramdas and Ruodu Wang (2025). The book gives a unified treatment of e-values and e-processes, including their use in anytime-valid inference and multiple testing. It is also available from the [publisher](https://doi.org/10.1561/3600000002).

**[Algorithmic Learning in a Random World](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-06649-8)** — Vladimir Vovk, Alexander Gammerman, and Glenn Shafer (2022). The second edition of a book on conformal prediction and algorithmic learning.

**[Game-Theoretic Foundations for Probability and Finance](https://doi.org/10.1002/9781118548035)** — Glenn Shafer and Vladimir Vovk (2019). A comprehensive treatment of game-theoretic probability and finance.

**[Probability and Finance: It’s Only a Game!](https://doi.org/10.1002/0471249696)** — Glenn Shafer and Vladimir Vovk (2001). A book-length development of game-theoretic probability based on testing forecasts through nonnegative capital processes.

**[Probability and the Weighing of Evidence](https://archive.org/details/probabilityweigh0000good)** — I. J. Good (1950). A foundational book on evidential and Bayesian reasoning.

**[Sequential Analysis](https://books.google.com/books?id=0nREAAAAIAAJ)** — Abraham Wald (1947). Wald’s foundational book on sequential statistical methods.

## Notes and reviews

**[E-values](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E-values)** — Wikipedia overview of the definition, interpretation, history, construction, and uses of e-values.

**[E-values and Multiple Testing](https://statistique.cuso.ch/fileadmin/statistique/user_upload/CUSO_LectureNotes_v28aug.pdf)** — Rianne de Heide (2026), CUSO Summer School lecture notes.

**[Game-Theoretic Statistics and Safe Anytime-Valid Inference](https://doi.org/10.1214/23-STS894)** — Aaditya Ramdas, Peter Grünwald, Vladimir Vovk, and Glenn Shafer (*Statistical Science*, 2023). A review of e-values, e-processes, confidence sequences, testing by betting, and the broader safe anytime-valid inference framework.

**[E-values (E for Expectation) in Hypothesis Testing](https://link.springer.com/rwe/10.1007/978-3-662-69359-9_189)** — Ruodu Wang (2025), entry in the *International Encyclopedia of Statistical Science*. A concise introduction to e-values as an alternative to p-values.
